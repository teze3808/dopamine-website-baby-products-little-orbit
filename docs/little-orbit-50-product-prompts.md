# Little Orbit — 50 Product Image Prompts

Use the shared art-direction block together with one numbered product block for each image. Generate each product as a separate square asset; do not request all products in one image.

## Shared art direction

```text
Use case: product-mockup
Asset type: square ecommerce catalog image for Little Orbit
Scene/backdrop: a dreamy midnight-cobalt handcrafted paper cosmos with a softly glowing cream moon, layered stitched clouds, tiny warm-gold stars, and one or two restrained celestial details that support the product
Style/medium: premium handcrafted storybook product illustration combining tactile fabric or natural materials, layered cut paper, embroidered details, and soft clay-like 3D celestial forms; sophisticated and cohesive, not generic cartoon clipart
Composition/framing: square catalog composition, one clear hero product or one clearly unified product set centered and fully visible, generous breathing room, clean readable silhouette, no cropped product edges
Lighting/mood: gentle moon glow, comforting bedtime magic, polished ecommerce presentation, soft dimensional shadows
Color palette: midnight cobalt, warm cream, sage green, butter yellow, muted coral, dusty blue, natural wood, restrained warm gold
Constraints: no infant or person, no hands, no text, no lettering, no logo, no brand marks, no watermark, no price tag, no packaging, no medical or safety claims, no realistic retailer resemblance
```

## Consistency checklist

- Generate one numbered product at a time using the shared block.
- Keep the backdrop dark cobalt and the product brighter than its surroundings.
- Maintain a single centered product or one clearly unified set.
- Preserve the same moon, stitched-cloud, paper-grain, and soft-shadow language.
- Never add people, hands, copy, packaging, logos, certification marks, or prices.
- If an output drifts, regenerate with: `Keep the product unchanged; simplify the background; restore the Little Orbit midnight-paper cosmos, stitched clouds, warm moonlight, and restrained palette.`
